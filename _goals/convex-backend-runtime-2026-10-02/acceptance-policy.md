## Mandatory acceptance

| Dimension | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Requirement fulfillment | 4/5: all mandatory product behavior met with clear evidence |
| Test quality | 4/5: meaningful outcomes and relevant negative/failure paths |
| Completeness | 4/5: reachable, usable API/client/configuration/installation |
| Code quality | 3/5: acceptable maintainability; nonbreaking debt disclosed |
| Pattern adherence | 3/5: reasonable deviations allowed |

These minima imply at least 3.6/5. No average overrides a blocker. Planning may pass at average 3.5 with no dimension below 3; missing critical dependencies or untestable mandatory behavior still block their dependent work. All product changes receive one non-author review of a coherent source-and-test batch; reviewers may be reused. One final independent audit must verify mandatory product requirements and zero unresolved breaking findings. Do not create separate judgments for routine receipts, preparation, data aliases, screenshots or handoffs.

A breaking finding includes observed or concretely reachable authorization bypass, cross-tenant/space access, secret exposure, wrong-target mutation, data loss/corruption/resurrection, duplicate paid/visible effects, bypassed model/budget controls, unsafe cancellation/retry, normal-use hang/leak, unusable public API/installation or broken supported compatibility. Fix it with a relevant regression before accepting the affected behavior. Tests reduce risk; completion never claims that all possible undiscovered defects have been mathematically excluded.
