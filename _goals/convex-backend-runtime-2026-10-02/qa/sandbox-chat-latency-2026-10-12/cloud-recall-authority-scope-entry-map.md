Exact eligible methods are the specialized AuthorityReader's principal lookup/get, membership lookup/get, grant get/list, tenant/space scope lookup and exact-key tombstone lookup, backed only by the five runtimeAuth control tables. Preserve complete raw records, list order/duplicates/ambiguity and absence results; replay original capability/reference/version/epoch/expiry/resource predicates on every call with its current time. Do not cache resolved authority, resource/source/managed-currentness predicates, semantic rows, budget/profile/policy decisions, arbitrary database selectors or ranges. The same context view must reach createAuthorityReader in runtimeDataAuth and runtimeDelivery as well as repository/model helpers; constructor-only scoping would split that reuse.

Mutation safety requires every insert/patch/replace/delete to invalidate before and after, including non-auth writes, and to disable retention while a write is in flight. Child/component runMutation also invalidates conservatively because the parent cannot infer its writes. A helper retaining the original unwrapped ctx can bypass this guarantee; all owned paths must receive the view. Query runQuery remains a same-read-snapshot child but creates its own invocation; do not transport the scope through RPC arguments or process state. On a native OCC retry the handler constructs a new scope. Current clock/deadline predicates must not reuse a prior decision even without database changes.

Reuse existing focused groups: runtimeAuth authority/provisioning/inventory plus new raw-read-scope tests; runtimeMemory repository/profiles/services/public-boundary/public-functional/vector-delivery-batch; runtimeIngestion context/context-permissions/cached-compatibility; runtimeAgent context-source-composition/context-latency/resolution-composition/model-owner/checkpoint-composition; runtime gateway-backend/gateway-dispatch/memory-gateway/model-policy-admission/model-policy-model-drift/profile-policy-selection. Required regression classes are same-snapshot repeated raw reads versus unchanged outputs/error order; duplicate or absent records; time-only expiry; detached mutable returned records; new invocation revocation; parent and child write invalidation; in-flight read/write race; unchanged per-vector/source guards and final-delivery refusal. No broad test rerun or service call was performed for this map.
