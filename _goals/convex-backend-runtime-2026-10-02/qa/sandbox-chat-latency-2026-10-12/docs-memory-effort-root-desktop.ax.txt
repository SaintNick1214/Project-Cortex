				137 text What a hop means
				138 link Description: Direct link to What a hop means, Value: 127.0.0.1:3367/core-features/memory-effort#what-a-hop-means
			139 text The current request is depth 0. A directly relevant memory, fact or approved pending source is depth 1, even if it is years old. Age and position in the transcript do not add hops. Another meaningful relation from that evidence adds one hop: for example, an authorized fact's source message, evidence derived from that source, or an existing explicitly related entity. Loading a record, checking authority or joining its provenance does not add a hop.
			140 text Only current, authorized, evidence-backed relations participate. Two records are not related merely because they share a conversation. Cortex deduplicates discovered evidence and retains its shortest permitted path rather than revisiting cycles. Convex-backed evidence links do not require an external graph database; unavailable external-only coverage is reported rather than fabricated.

Required content that cannot fit refuses before another model call.
