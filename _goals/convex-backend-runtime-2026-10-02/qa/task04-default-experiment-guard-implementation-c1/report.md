## Monetary uncertainty and lifetime are distinct

This implements preparation report19–23,64,68–74,80 and fresh review's Important implementation gates. Monetary qualification and lifetime attestation are separate:

- A supported genuine terminal attestation releases that slot **once**, even when monetary currency/category/hypothesis validation is absent or fails. Missing money with otherwise supported hypothesis/category/usage and a genuine terminal may allow the next fixed serial permit while full K remains cumulative. This matches “Captured genuine terminal plus missing money may release slot once and retain K” in preparation report64. It does not assert free charges or replace unknown cost with a rate estimate.

Overrun latches stop; amounts are never clipped to K or engineering limits. Known money with unknown lifetime accounts money but retains its active slot; a compatible later genuine terminal releases the slot without another charge.
